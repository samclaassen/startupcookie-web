Engagements from $5,000/mo, month-to-month, no long contracts. The output of a full content team at the price of a junior hire. What we run for clients

A complete thought leadership program: voice card built from the founder's existing best content, topic ladder mapped to commercial-intent AEO queries, weekly cadence of 3-5 LinkedIn posts and 1-2 long-form articles, monthly webinar with full repurposing into 20-30 assets, quarterly case studies with named customers and hard numbers, and weekly distribution review with performance attribution. We handle production; the founder provides 1-2 hours per week of input.

Who this is for

  1. Seed to Series B B2B SaaS founders who know their content is not working and do not have time to fix it themselves.
  2. Growth-stage marketing leaders without an in-house content team yet who need the program to compound while they build the rest of the marketing function.
  3. Series A and B founders who want to bring production in-house eventually and need a partner to run the program while we build them a custom content agent.

Who this is not for

  • B2C, e-commerce, or consumer brands. We are calibrated for B2B SaaS specifically.
  • Companies past Series C with established content teams. We are the bridge, not the replacement.
  • Founders who will not commit 1-2 hours per week to input calls. Programs without founder input flatten into AI-generic content.

Frequently asked questions

How much founder time does this require?

1-2 hours per week. 30-60 minutes of audio input (recorded on a phone, on a Loom, or on a call with us), plus 15-30 minutes of weekly draft review.

How fast does this start working?

First trust signals (inbound DMs, AI citations): 4-8 weeks. Measurable pipeline lift: 90-120 days. Compounding effects on CAC: 6-12 months. Programs that stop before 90 days never compound.

Do you handle distribution or only production?

Both. Distribution is half the work. We run LinkedIn distribution (founder + company), email amplification, paid amplification on the highest-performing posts, and AEO/SEO optimization on every long-form article.

Can we keep our existing content writer?

Yes. We slot in alongside an existing freelance writer or in-house marketer. We typically take strategy, AEO/SEO optimization, and distribution; your existing writer can continue handling drafting if that works for the team.

What is the difference between this and your content agent service?

The thought leadership program is a managed service; we run the content. The content agent service is a custom buildout; we build the agent and your team operates it. Many clients run both: we run the program while we build the agent, then the team takes over once the agent is shipped.
