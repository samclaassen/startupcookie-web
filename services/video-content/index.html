FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What footage do you need from us?

Whatever you already have. Webinar and podcast recordings, Zoom exports, conference talks, product demos, and phone-recorded founder videos all work. Cleaner source footage gives us more to work with, but you do not need a studio.

What is the turnaround time?

For webinar repurposing, assets are ready to ship within 7 days of the live event. For other video work, turnaround depends on the length of the footage and the number of deliverables. We set the schedule together at kickoff.

Do you handle publishing and distribution?

We deliver publish-ready files, captioned and formatted for each platform. On webinar repurposing engagements, distribution is built into the program. If you want us to handle publishing beyond that, we scope it on the intro call.

How does pricing work?

Engagements from $5,000/mo, scoped to your goals. Month-to-month, no long contracts.

Who does the work?

StartupCookie is two people: co-founders Sam Claassen and Barbara Jovanovic. Senior-only, no account managers. The people you meet on the intro call are the people who edit your video.
