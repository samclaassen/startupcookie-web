What you get

Everything from voice card to published post

Voice card

We pull your existing best LinkedIn posts and articles, then extract the patterns: sentence length, opinion markers, vocabulary you do and do not use, exemplar posts. It is the single most leveraged artifact in the program; every draft is calibrated against it.

Topic ladder

3-5 topics you own and want to be known for. We post about those exclusively for 90 days. No "Monday motivation" posts. No off-topic viral grabs.

3-5 posts per week

Automated publishing

Approved posts go to LinkedIn at optimized times. You never log in to publish.

Weekly performance review

How it works

How the program runs

1Voice card and topic ladder

We build the voice card from your existing best posts and set the topic ladder: 3-5 topics you own and want to be known for, posted about exclusively for 90 days.

2Weekly audio call, drafts in 48 hours

30-45 minutes with you: this week's learnings, customer conversations, hot takes on industry news, behind-the-scenes operational notes. We record, transcribe, and draft 3-5 posts within 48 hours.

3You review, automation queues

15-20 minutes per batch. Edit in voice, kill posts that do not feel right. Approved posts go to LinkedIn at optimized times, and the weekly performance review keeps the voice card and topic ladder current.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How quickly will I see inbound from LinkedIn?

3-6 weeks for first inbound DMs and impression growth. 9-12 weeks for measurable pipeline (sourced demos). 12-18 weeks for compounding effects on CAC.

Do you write in my voice or your voice?

Your voice. The voice card is built from your real posts. Drafts that read like generic marketing copy never ship; the founder review step catches them.

What if I do not have existing LinkedIn content for the voice card?

We build the voice card from interview transcripts, podcast appearances, internal Slack messages, or any other source of your actual written or spoken voice. New-founder voice cards take an extra week to dial in.

Can I see drafts before they post?

Yes. Every draft is queued for your review. Nothing posts without your approval. You can review on a phone in 15-20 minutes per week.

Do you handle comment replies?

Optional add-on. We can draft comment replies in your voice for you to approve. We recommend the founder handles comments, because that is where pipeline-quality conversations start.
