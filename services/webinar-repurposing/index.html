What you get

What we ship per webinar

1 long-form blog post (1,500-2,000 words, AEO-built, with schema)

5-7 LinkedIn posts (each anchored to a key argument, calibrated to the speaker's voice card)

3-5 short-form videos (60-90 seconds each, cut from the highest-engagement moments)

1 highlight reel (90 seconds, ships with the replay link in the first 48 hours)

1-2 replay promotion posts (drive registrants and non-attendees to the replay)

1 podcast cut (audio-only edit, ready to publish to Apple Podcasts/Spotify)

1 email newsletter (top 3 takeaways with the replay link)

1 LinkedIn carousel (visual summary, 8-10 slides)

3-5 stat graphics (key numbers from the webinar, shareable on LinkedIn and X/Twitter)

Optional: 1 case study (if a customer was on the webinar; requires customer signoff)

Total: 17-25 ready-to-ship assets per webinar, all delivered within 7 days of the live event.

How it works

From live event to shipped assets in 7 days

1. Before and during the webinar

Pre-webinar, we agree on the repurposing plan: which segments to cut, which speakers to feature, and what the blog post angle is. On webinar day, we attend (live or recorded) to flag the highlight moments in real time.

2. First 48 hours

The replay link goes out with the 90-second highlight reel, LinkedIn posts from each speaker, an email to registrants, and 3 short-form videos.

3. Week 1-2 and distribution

We ship the blog post, podcast cut, carousel, stat graphics, and additional LinkedIn posts. Distribution runs on LinkedIn (speakers plus company page), paid amplification on the top-performing posts, and an email push to your list.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to run our own webinars or can you run them for us?

You run them; we repurpose. Webinar hosting (platform, registration flow, live moderation) is outside our scope. We can recommend platforms (Hubilo, Restream, On24, or a simpler Zoom + StreamYard setup), but the webinar itself is yours.

How fast do the repurposed assets ship after the webinar?

First 48 hours: replay link, highlight reel, LinkedIn posts from speakers, email to registrants, 3 short-form videos. Within 7 days: blog post, podcast cut, carousel, stat graphics, additional LinkedIn posts.

What if our webinar has confidential content or NDA customer info?

We sign your standard NDA before kickoff. Assets that include NDA content go through your team's review before publishing. We never publish without your approval.

Can you handle the webinar production itself (video editing, captions, branding)?

Yes, as an add-on. Most clients handle production with their existing tools and we handle repurposing. We can recommend production partners or take it ourselves; pricing depends on complexity.

Does this work for podcasts instead of webinars?

Yes. Our 1:10 framework (one recording becomes ten-plus distribution-ready pieces, explained in our webinar repurposing guide) applies to any long-form audio or video content. Podcast episodes typically produce slightly fewer assets than webinars because the registration signal is absent, but the playbook is the same.
