FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I bundle multiple services?

Yes. Most clients run thought leadership plus AEO together. The bundled price is typically lower than the sum of standalone services; we will quote during the scoping call.

What is the minimum commitment?

Managed services run month-to-month. We recommend at least 90 days because content programs compound in the second and third month, not the first. Agent buildouts are one-time, fixed-scope projects, so there is no ongoing commitment unless you add an editorial retainer.

How does onboarding work?

2 weeks. Week 1: voice card kickoff, topic ladder, AEO target query selection, distribution setup. Week 2: first batch of content drafted and reviewed. Week 3 onwards: cadence. We do not require a 6-week setup phase.

Who is the team that will work on my account?

A senior content strategist owns each engagement, supported by AI-native production tooling and (when relevant) a junior editorial associate. One named human is your primary contact. No account managers, no junior writers running the program.

What does the GTM & content agent service have to do with the managed services?

The GTM & content agent service is a custom buildout; we deliver an agent that your team operates. The managed services are us running the content for you. Many clients run both: we run the managed service for 90-180 days while we build the agent, then your team takes over and we stay on retainer for editorial review.

Do you work with B2C, e-commerce, or consumer brands?

No. Every service is calibrated for B2B SaaS. The voice cards, AEO playbooks, distribution channels, and buyer-journey assumptions are all built around B2B buying cycles. We refer B2C teams to category-appropriate specialists.
