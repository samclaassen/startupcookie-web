Fractional CMO for Seed-Stage B2B Startups

By Barbara Jovanovic at StartupCookie | Last updated: November 2026
One person, month-to-month

The short version: StartupCookie founder Barbara Jovanovic works as a fractional CMO for seed-stage B2B startups that have no marketing hire. She takes the role a first marketing hire would fill and does the work herself: founder LinkedIn, customer stories, launch comms, and media and speaking decisions. StartupCookie is a one-person agency, so the person on the intro call is the person who does the work. The public example is Juno, an AI-moderated research platform backed by Blackbird, where the engagement ran through January 2026.

The proof: Juno

Juno is seed stage: Nick Crocker at Blackbird led a $2.8M round. The company had no marketing hire, so every podcast invite, speaking request, customer launch and market-entry decision landed on the CEO's desk. The engagement grew past content into the role a first marketing hire would fill.

It ran through January 2026, when Juno brought marketing operations in-house. A fractional CMO covers the role until the company hires for it. Read the Juno case study.

Who it fits

  • Seed-stage B2B startups with no marketing hire, where marketing decisions land on the CEO.
  • Founder-led companies whose buyers want to hear from the people building the product.
  • Teams that want one senior person to decide and to do the work, month-to-month.

Who should hire someone else

  • You need a full-time executive in the company every day. Hire in-house. The trade-offs are in the agency vs in-house comparison.
  • Your first need is paid acquisition. StartupCookie's services are content-led: founder LinkedIn, thought leadership, product marketing, AEO, video and GTM agents. Paid media is not on the service list.
  • You need someone to run an enterprise-scale, multi-team program. StartupCookie is one person with an eight-client cap.
  • You sell to consumers. StartupCookie works with B2B companies only.

Where AEO fits

Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI which product to use. The work a first marketing hire would do. At StartupCookie that means founder and executive content, customer stories, launch comms, media and speaking decisions, and the content side of a market entry. Barbara decides what to do and also does it.

How is a fractional CMO different from a marketing agency?

A fractional CMO owns marketing decisions as well as deliverables. An agency usually delivers a scoped set of assets. StartupCookie does both through one person: Barbara sets the plan and produces the work, and custom AI agents handle the repeatable production.
