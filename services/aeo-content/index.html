The checklist

The 7 elements we ship on every page

Element 1

Extractable lede

120-180 words that answer the page's primary question in the first 30 words.

Element 2

Answer-first H2s

Each one a real query, each one answered in 60 words or fewer.

Element 3

Named entities

Specific company names, real products, real cited stats.

Element 4

Sourced statistics

Every stat traces to a URL the reader can click.

Element 5

Tables and numbered lists

Comparison tables and numbered lists, both over-represented in cited answers.

Element 6

FAQ with FAQPage schema

A visible FAQ section whose HTML matches the schema text exactly.

Element 7

Freshness signals

Year in the title, a visible last-updated tag, and schema dateModified set to today.

Every page we ship hits all 7 elements and validates against the AEO linter before it goes out.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long until we see AI citations?

14-28 days for Perplexity, 21-45 days for ChatGPT, 28-56 days for Google AI Overviews, 28-60 days for Claude. Anything faster is unusual; anything longer than 90 days suggests the topic does not have real query volume or the pages are missing one of the 7 elements.

What is the difference between AEO and SEO?

SEO optimizes for Google's ranked search-results page. AEO targets the answer an AI assistant generates. The signals overlap but weight differently. See our AEO guide for the full breakdown.

Do you do AEO on existing pages or only new ones?

Both. We typically audit your existing top 10-20 pages first, fix what is leaking citations (usually schema, lede, FAQ matching), then add new pages on the queries you do not yet rank for.

Can you guarantee citations?

No, and anyone who does is lying. We can guarantee that every page we ship hits the 7 elements above and validates against the AEO linter. The citation outcome depends on real query volume and the competitive landscape, which we cannot fully control.

What citation tracker do you use?

We start new clients on a lightweight tracker covering a starter set of prompts, then expand coverage as the program ramps. For larger programs we run a DIY tracker across Perplexity Sonar, OpenAI's web search, Anthropic's web search, and Google Gemini grounding. The DIY tracker runs on a small monthly API budget and lives in your stack, not ours.
