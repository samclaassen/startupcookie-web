How we work

Agents do the production. Humans do the judgment.

Barbara runs every engagement herself. One senior founder, a deliberate client cap, and no layers in between.

AI-native by design

I build custom agents that handle the repeatable production work. That leverage is how one person ships the output of a full content team, without hiring one.

Senior-only, no account managers

Every strategy call, edit, and decision comes from the founder. Nothing gets handed off to a junior team, because there is no junior team.

Maximum six clients at a time

I cap the roster at six clients. The cap keeps me close to every account, and it is why the model works.

Simple terms

You can stop any month, with no long-term commitment. Onboarding takes 2 weeks from kickoff to first published asset. You give 1-2 hours per week of input, and programs compound in 90-120 days.
