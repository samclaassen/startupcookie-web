Venn: Founder-Led Content for a Canadian Fintech

By Barbara Jovanovic at StartupCookie | Last updated: July 2026 | All case studies

Founder LinkedIn for co-founder Ahmed Shafik (858 reactions and 48 comments on the flagship post), company video, and the Stan customer story, all live and linked.

Venn (venn.ca) is business banking built for Canada: CAD and USD accounts with CDIC protection, corporate cards, and low-cost foreign exchange. The company's site says it supports 10,000+ Canadian businesses. In February 2025, Venn announced a $21.5M CAD Series A led by Left Lane Capital and rebranded from Vault to Venn (BetaKit, Feb 26, 2025). StartupCookie produces founder LinkedIn content for co-founder Ahmed Shafik, video content for the Venn company page, and written customer case studies. Every deliverable in this case study is live, public, and linked.

Disclosure: StartupCookie is our agency. This case study describes our own work for Venn. Every engagement number below is public on LinkedIn and linked to its source.

The challenge

A rebrand resets recognition. In February 2025, Vault became Venn on the same day the company announced its Series A. That put Venn in a common but uncomfortable spot: a new name, a fresh round, and a product roadmap that needed the team's full attention.

What a Series A fintech needs in market at that moment is simple to state. It needs a founder voice that Canadian founders and finance leaders recognize. And it needs public proof that real customers run real money through the product. Venn brought in StartupCookie to build both while the team built product.

What we did

Founder LinkedIn for Ahmed Shafik

We produce LinkedIn content in Ahmed's voice. The flagship post opens with a question he hears constantly: "Everyone asks why I'm building in Canada". It makes the case for building the company at home, from the founder himself, not from a brand account.

Video content on the Venn company page

We produce short video content for Venn's LinkedIn company page. One example opens on a pain every operator knows: still waiting half the week for your bank. Video gives the brand a face between founder posts.

A written customer case study

We wrote "How Stan simplified global payments for its creator platform with Venn", published Nov 17, 2025 on venn.ca. It documents how Stan, a creator platform, uses Venn for global payments. Customer proof, in the customer's own context, on Venn's own domain.

Results

All of the work above is live. Here are the public numbers, as shown on LinkedIn in July 2026:

  • The flagship founder post has 858 reactions and 48 comments (view the post).
  • The video content is live on Venn's LinkedIn company page.
  • The Stan case study is published on venn.ca.
