Puzzle: Executive Thought Leadership for an AI Accounting Platform

By Barbara Jovanovic at StartupCookie | Last updated: July 2026 | All case studies

Executive long-form under CEO Sasha Orloff's byline and the Paradigm (YC W24) customer case study, both live on puzzle.io.

Puzzle (puzzle.io) is AI accounting software. The company describes it as "a modern ledger that delivers real-time, accurate financials while keeping accountants firmly in control" (puzzle.io/about). Sasha Orloff is CEO and co-founder. In November 2023, Puzzle announced a $30M raise, bringing total funding to $50M at the time (Puzzle announcement, Nov 14, 2023). StartupCookie produces executive long-form content under Sasha Orloff's byline and customer case studies for the Puzzle blog. Both deliverables below are live, public, and linked.

Disclosure: StartupCookie is our agency. This case study describes our own work for Puzzle. Every deliverable below is public on puzzle.io and linked to its source.

The challenge

AI accounting software asks accountants to trust a system that touches their core work. Puzzle's answer to that tension sits in its own product description: real-time, accurate financials, with accountants firmly in control.

A position like that cannot live only on a homepage. It carries more weight when the CEO argues it in public, in long form, under his own name. And it needs customer proof: named companies describing how they actually run their financials on the product.

That is the engagement. StartupCookie produces executive content under Sasha Orloff's byline and customer case studies on the Puzzle blog, so the positioning has a voice and the product has proof.

What we did

Executive long-form under Sasha Orloff's byline

We produced "Our Continued Commitment to Accounting Firms", published Oct 28, 2025 on the Puzzle blog. It puts the company's position on accounting firms in the CEO's own voice, on Puzzle's own domain, where firms evaluating the product will look first.

A written customer case study

We wrote "How Paradigm (YC W24) Simplified Financial Management with Puzzle", published Feb 4, 2025. It documents how Paradigm, a Y Combinator W24 company, uses Puzzle to manage its financials. A named customer, a real workflow, on the record.

Results

Both pieces are live on puzzle.io:

  • "Our Continued Commitment to Accounting Firms", executive content under Sasha Orloff's byline, published Oct 28, 2025.
  • "How Paradigm (YC W24) Simplified Financial Management with Puzzle", customer case study, published Feb 4, 2025.

One honest note on metrics. Blog posts on a company domain do not carry public engagement counters the way LinkedIn posts do, so there are no reaction counts to cite here. The verifiable results are the published pieces themselves. Business-outcome numbers from this engagement publish here once they are verified and Puzzle signs off.
