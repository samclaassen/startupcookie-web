StartupCookie vs Omniscient Digital

By Barbara Jovanovic at StartupCookie | Last updated: July 2026
Honest comparison, sources linked

The short version: these are two good agencies built on different models. Omniscient Digital is an organic growth agency that helps marketing leaders at ambitious B2B brands turn SEO, GEO, and content into growth channels. It is strategy-led, runs a distributed specialist team, and publishes a starting price of $10,000 per month for full-service work. StartupCookie is an AI-native content & GTM agency for B2B SaaS. It is a two-person senior-only team, capped at six clients, with engagements from $5,000/mo and AEO (answer engine optimization, i.e. getting cited by AI search tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity) as a core program rather than an add-on. If you want a deep SEO strategy engine with a bigger team behind it, pick Omniscient. If you want senior hands-on production, AI-search focus, and a lower entry price, pick StartupCookie.

Disclosure: StartupCookie is our agency, so read this page knowing who wrote it. Every claim about Omniscient Digital comes from their own public website and is linked next to the claim. We compare models, not quality, and we say plainly where Omniscient is the better choice.

Who each agency is best for

Omniscient Digital fits B2B software companies that want a strategic partner for organic growth. Their positioning is explicit: SEO, GEO, and content should drive business growth, not just traffic, and revenue is the end goal (source). The client list on their homepage includes names like Jasper, Order.co, Smartling, and TikTok Shop (source), which tells you the typical client is funded and already has a marketing function. If you have a marketing leader who wants a research-heavy roadmap and a team of specialists executing it, that is their lane.

StartupCookie fits earlier. We work with seed to Series B B2B SaaS founders who do not have a content team yet and want the whole thing run for them: strategy, production, and distribution. Our AEO content program ships 4-8 AEO-built pages per month with weekly citation tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. The founder gives 1-2 hours per week of input and we handle the rest. We do not work with B2C, e-commerce, or consumer brands.

What Omniscient Digital does

Omniscient Digital was founded by David Ly Khim, Alex Birkett, and Allie Konchar, all former HubSpot marketers with later stops at People.ai, Workato, and Shopify (their about page). The service menu covers SEO and content strategy, generative engine optimization, programmatic SEO, technical SEO, content production, digital PR and link building, and conversion rate optimization (source).

Two things stand out about their approach. First, the strategy work is genuinely deep: their SEO and content strategy package runs buyer, product, and channel research, includes LLM visibility indexing and an AI search roadmap, and starts at $15,000 as a standalone project (source). Second, they think in portfolios. Their published barbell content strategy balances predictable product-led content against a smaller share of experimental, attention-grabbing pieces. That is a real editorial philosophy, not a slogan.

They also publish specific client results, including 810% organic session growth for Jasper and $3.7M in pipeline for Smartling (their homepage case studies). We have not verified those numbers independently, but they are public, named, and specific, which is more than most agencies offer.

What StartupCookie does

StartupCookie is an AI-native content & GTM agency for B2B SaaS. AI-native means the production layer runs on AI systems we built, with the two of us (co-founders Sam Claassen and Barbara Jovanovic) doing all strategy and editorial work ourselves. There are no account managers and no handoffs. That model only works with a hard cap, so we take a maximum of six clients at a time.

The service set: thought leadership content, founder LinkedIn, AEO / AI search content, product marketing, video content, and GTM and content agents. The AEO program is the closest overlap with Omniscient's work, and it is where we have our strongest public proof: our client Tofu grew AI search visibility 2.36x in 90 days (7.00% to 16.51%), and tofuhq.com became the #2 most-cited domain in Marketing Technology, ahead of HubSpot, YouTube, and Reddit. The full breakdown is in the Tofu case study. If you want the underlying playbook, our AEO for B2B SaaS guide walks through it.

The model comparison

This is the real difference between the two agencies, so it is worth being precise.

Team structure. Omniscient runs a distributed team organized into specialist divisions: growth strategy and SEO, editorial strategy and creative, outreach and digital PR, client success, and client operations (source). You get depth in each function and an account team coordinating it. StartupCookie is two senior people. You get the same two brains on strategy, production, and distribution, and you talk to the people doing the work. Neither model is better in the abstract. Specialist teams scale further; a two-person senior team moves faster and loses nothing in handoffs.

Production. Omniscient's engine is research-led editorial: strategy first, then content production tied to the roadmap. StartupCookie's engine is AI-native: custom agents handle drafting, repurposing, and formatting, and the co-founders do the thinking and the editing. In practice that means a smaller team ships full program volume, and onboarding runs 2 weeks from kickoff to first published asset.

Search philosophy. Omniscient comes from SEO and has expanded into GEO (generative engine optimization, their term for the same discipline as AEO). It is one service inside a broader organic growth program. StartupCookie starts from AEO: pages are built for extraction and citation by AI answer engines first, with classic SEO hygiene included rather than the other way around. If most of your buyers still start in Google's blue links, Omniscient's center of gravity helps you. If your buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT or Perplexity which tool to buy, ours does.

Capacity. We cap at six clients; Omniscient's site does not mention a client cap. A cap costs us revenue and buys focus. A larger roster funds a deeper bench. Pick the trade-off you prefer.

Pricing

Only published numbers here. Omniscient Digital states that full-service engagements start at $10,000 a month, and that their standalone SEO and content strategy package starts at $15,000 (source). Beyond those anchors, pricing is not published; their site invites you to book a strategy call to scope the work.

StartupCookie: engagements from $5,000/mo, month-to-month, no long contracts. The AEO program starts at $5,000/mo for 4 AEO-built pages per month plus citation tracking, and runs $10,000-$15,000/mo for 6-8 pages with the full tracker. Thought leadership starts at $5,000/mo standalone, $10,000-$15,000/mo as a full-stack program.

One honest note on comparing these numbers: a $10,000 Omniscient engagement and a $10,000 StartupCookie engagement buy different things. Theirs buys a specialist team and a research-heavy SEO program. Ours buys two senior operators, AI-native production volume, and an AI-search-first program. Cheaper is not automatically better; match the spend to the channel you believe in.

When to choose Omniscient Digital

  • You want a strategy partner, not just production. Their $15,000 strategy package with buyer, product, and channel research is a genuine consulting deliverable (source).
  • Classic SEO is still your main channel. Technical SEO, programmatic SEO, digital PR, and link building are on their menu and not on ours.
  • You are later stage with a marketing leader in place. Their public client list (Jasper, Smartling, TikTok Shop) suggests the model works best with an internal owner to receive the strategy.
  • You want a bigger bench. Specialist divisions mean no single person is a bottleneck.

When to choose StartupCookie

  • AI search visibility is the goal. AEO is our core program, with weekly citation tracking and a public before-and-after result in the Tofu case study.
  • You are seed to Series B without a content team. We run strategy, production, and distribution end to end for 1-2 hours of founder input per week.
  • You want seniors doing the work. Two co-founders, no account managers, maximum six clients.
  • Budget starts at $5,000/mo. That is our published entry point, month-to-month, versus their published $10,000/mo full-service floor.
  • You eventually want the system in-house. We build GTM and content agents as one-time projects, so the machine can transfer to your team.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main difference between StartupCookie and Omniscient Digital?

The core difference is focus and model. Omniscient Digital is an organic growth agency built around SEO, GEO, and content strategy for B2B software, run by a distributed team with specialist divisions. StartupCookie is an AI-native content and GTM agency for B2B SaaS, run by two senior co-founders with no account managers, capped at six clients, with AEO (answer engine optimization, getting cited by AI search tools) as a core service. Omniscient leans strategy-heavy and search-led; StartupCookie leans production-heavy and AI-search-led.

How much does Omniscient Digital cost?

Omniscient Digital publishes two price anchors on its own site: full-service engagements start at $10,000 a month, and its SEO and content strategy development package starts at $15,000. Beyond those anchors pricing is not published; their site invites a strategy call to scope the work.

How much does StartupCookie cost?

Engagements from $5,000/mo, month-to-month with no long contracts. The AEO program starts at $5,000/mo for 4 AEO-built pages per month plus citation tracking, and runs $10,000-$15,000/mo for 6-8 pages with a full tracker.

Which agency is better for an early-stage B2B SaaS startup?

It depends on budget and what you need first. If you have $10,000+ per month and want a deep SEO and organic growth strategy with a specialist team behind it, Omniscient Digital is a strong choice. If you are seed to Series B, want senior hands-on production without account managers, and care most about showing up in AI search answers, StartupCookie starts at half the published entry price and is built for that stage.

Does Omniscient Digital do AEO?

Yes, under the name GEO (generative engine optimization). Omniscient Digital lists generative engine optimization among its services and includes LLM visibility indexing and an AI search roadmap in its strategy work. AEO and GEO describe the same discipline: getting your pages cited in answers from tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The difference is emphasis: for Omniscient it is one service inside a broader SEO program, while for StartupCookie it is a core program with weekly citation tracking.
