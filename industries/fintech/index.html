What's different

What fintech content has to do differently.

Trust comes before features

Founders and finance leaders move money with companies they already recognize. Venn's program is built on a co-founder people know from their feed and a company account that shows real customers using the product.

Proof has to be checkable

Fintech buyers discount anonymous claims. Customer stories name the company and show real money moving through the product: Venn's run on venn.ca under the customers' own names, and Puzzle's Paradigm story is on the record on puzzle.io.

Finance teams want control, not magic

Software that touches the ledger asks accountants to trust it with their core work. Puzzle's content argues its position in the CEO's own words, in long form, on its own domain: real-time, accurate financials with accountants firmly in control.

Self-serve has to sell without a sales call

Venn grows through self-serve signup with no sales calls, so the content does the selling: YouTube videos that answer what Canadian business owners already search, launch content for every feature, and onboarding emails that take a new signup to the first transaction.
