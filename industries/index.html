FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What industries does StartupCookie work with?

B2B companies, mostly seed to Series B. Most clients are fintech and finance software companies, such as Venn and Puzzle, or AI startups, such as Juno and Tofu. Others include MBpractice in health tech, AIKO in security, SEOmonitor in SEO software, and the agencies Propane and Acaisoft.

Do you only work with SaaS companies?

No. Most clients sell software, but StartupCookie also works with agencies and consultancies that sell to businesses, such as Propane and Acaisoft.

Do you work with B2C or e-commerce brands?

No. StartupCookie works with B2B companies only.

Who does the work?

Founder Barbara Jovanovic runs every engagement herself, and custom AI agents handle the production work. There are no account managers, and StartupCookie takes a maximum of eight clients at a time.
