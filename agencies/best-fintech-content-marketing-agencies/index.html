The Best Fintech Content Marketing Agencies in 2026

By Barbara Jovanovic at StartupCookie | Last updated: November 2026
Independently researched, sources linked

The short version: nine agencies cover the useful range for fintech content in 2026. StartupCookie, Mint Studios, Block Club, Fintech Content, Content Rewired, Beyond the Arc, Siege Media, NoGood and Optimist all show fintech work you can check on their own sites, but they solve different problems. Mint Studios, Fintech Content and Content Rewired work only in fintech and financial services. Block Club rebuilds brand and positioning for fintech infrastructure. Beyond the Arc writes for fintechs that sell to banks. Siege Media and NoGood run search and growth programs at scale, and Optimist sells SEO and AEO strategy to teams that write in-house. StartupCookie (our agency, disclosure below) runs founder LinkedIn, customer stories, launches and video for early-stage B2B fintech.

Disclosure: StartupCookie is our agency. We include ourselves in this list alongside real alternatives for transparency. Every entry follows the same rules: claims about other agencies come from their own public websites, pricing appears only where it is published, and limitations are listed for everyone, including us.

What a fintech content agency should do

Fintech buyers move money with companies they already trust, so the job is different from generic B2B content. A good fintech content agency builds recognition for the founders, publishes customer stories that name the company and show real money moving through the product, and argues the company's position in long form where finance teams will check it. For self-serve products, it also has to convert without a sales call: launch content, onboarding emails and search-friendly video. Ask every agency on this list for named fintech work you can open and read.

How we evaluated this list

We started from the agencies that AI assistants name when asked for a fintech content marketing agency, then checked each one's own website on November 10, 2026 for three things: named fintech clients or case studies, the content services it actually lists, and published pricing. We kept the agencies with work we could verify. Every claim about another agency links to the page it came from, and where pricing isn't public we say so instead of guessing.

1. StartupCookie: founder LinkedIn, customer stories and launches for B2B fintech

StartupCookie is a content and GTM agency run by founder Barbara Jovanovic, with custom AI agents handling production and a cap of eight clients at a time. Its fintech work is public. Since August 2025 it has run Venn's founder LinkedIn, company account, YouTube channel, launches and customer stories. For two years it produced executive long-form, customer case studies and podcast support for Puzzle, the AI accounting platform. More on StartupCookie's fintech work.

  • Best for: seed to Series B B2B fintech startups that want founder-led content, customer stories and launches from one senior operator.
  • Pricing: engagements from $5,000/mo, month-to-month.
  • Fintech proof: Venn co-founder Ahmed Shafik's LinkedIn closed 2025 at 1,192,605 impressions, up 405.2% on the prior year, written by StartupCookie since August 2025; five named customer stories live on venn.ca; Puzzle's CEO long-form and Paradigm customer story on puzzle.io.
  • Strengths: founder LinkedIn, video, launches and customer stories under one roof; named, checkable fintech proof; a transparent starting price.
  • Limitations: B2B only, so no consumer fintech. The eight-client cap limits capacity. No paid media. Conversion results for Venn's launches aren't published.
  • Verdict: a strong fit for early-stage B2B fintech that wants its founder to be the face of the company and needs customer proof to back it. Not the pick for consumer fintech or paid acquisition.
Disclosure (repeated): StartupCookie is our agency, so treat this entry with appropriate skepticism and check us against the alternatives below.

2. Mint Studios: pipeline-focused content for fintech and financial services

Mint Studios, at mintcopywritingstudios.com, describes itself as "a content marketing agency that turns content into pipeline for fintech and financial services companies." Its services center on content strategy, expert-interview articles, fintech SEO, LLM visibility and attribution dashboards. Published case studies include Fiska, WorldFirst and Yapily, and its About page names Modulr, Persona and Confused.com among clients.

  • Best for: fintech and financial-services companies that want expert-interview content plus SEO and LLM visibility, measured against pipeline.
  • Pricing: published, but the range differs by page: "between USD 5,000 - USD 20,000 per month" on the homepage, "$8,000 - $20,000" on What We Do and "$10,000 - $20,000" on Our Method.
  • Fintech proof: Yapily: "2.8x Growth in Organic Inbound Website Leads"; WorldFirst: "50+ conversions per month".
  • Strengths: fintech-only focus, named fintech case studies with numbers, attribution to pipeline.
  • Limitations: its What We Do page lists no founder or executive LinkedIn ghostwriting.
  • Verdict: the most focused fintech content specialist on this list. Pair it with someone else if you also need founder LinkedIn.

3. Block Club: brand, positioning and content systems for fintech infrastructure

Block Club says it builds "AI-ready brands and content systems that are impossible to ignore." Its fintech page lists positioning, content systems and brand identities for Plaid, Alloy, Argyle, Codat, Middesk, Pigment and PingPong. In its Plaid case study, "Plaid attributed $25 million in closed opportunities to the content we produced." It works with companies "from Series A onward."

  • Best for: Series A+ B2B fintech infrastructure companies (payments, identity and fraud, data) that need positioning and brand work plus an ongoing content system.
  • Pricing: not published.
  • Fintech proof: Plaid ($25 million in closed opportunities attributed to content), Alloy ("Organic search traffic has grown 52% since 2024"), Argyle (157% more blog views in nine months).
  • Strengths: a deep fintech-infrastructure roster, brand and content under one roof, engagements led by senior directors.
  • Limitations: brand-first rather than demand gen: asked about demand generation and paid media, its FAQ answers "Not directly." Pricing isn't public.
  • Verdict: the pick for fintech infrastructure companies that need their positioning and brand rebuilt along with the content.

4. Fintech Content: a fintech-only writing team

Fintech Content is, in its own words, "a London based content agency for fintech and financial services companies, founded in 2020 by Deborah Boyland" (source). Its services cover blog and web writing, content strategy, eBooks and whitepapers. Case studies include Spreedly, FundGuard and RiskSmart. Its process page says it writes "between 2 and 8 articles per month."

  • Best for: fintech marketing teams that want a steady cadence of specialist blogs, whitepapers and eBooks.
  • Pricing: not published.
  • Fintech proof: FundGuard's review says the blog program "helped to triple our website and social media engagement in the first year".
  • Strengths: fintech-only writers, a clear monthly cadence, named case studies across payments, investment accounting and RegTech.
  • Limitations: a small, writing-led team: its About page lists three people, and its FAQ says "our core service is writing." Pricing isn't public.
  • Verdict: a good fit when you need reliable fintech writing more than strategy or video.

5. Content Rewired: founder-delivered fintech content strategy

Content Rewired promises "Fintech content built on craft, accountable to pipeline." It is run by founder Ashley Poynter in Chicago and describes itself as "A founder-led practice, not an agency" (source). Its PaymentWorks work reports that "Inbound leads grew 192% year over year, delivering nine closed deals worth $560K between January and September 2025." Typical clients are B2B fintechs "most often between Series A and Series C."

  • Best for: Series A to C B2B fintechs in payments, treasury, banking infrastructure or lending that want senior, founder-delivered strategy and editorial leadership.
  • Pricing: "starts at $7,500 for a fixed-scope sprint and $6,000 a month for an ongoing retainer," with early-stage pricing for smaller companies (source).
  • Fintech proof: PaymentWorks: inbound leads up 192% year over year, with nine closed deals worth $560K.
  • Strengths: published pricing, fintech-only focus, a measured pipeline result.
  • Limitations: a one-person practice by its own description; direct writing is offered "on a case-by-case basis" (source), and it books clients selectively.
  • Verdict: a strong pick if you want a senior fintech editor-in-chief rather than a production team.

6. Beyond the Arc: content for fintechs that sell to banks

Beyond the Arc describes itself as "a B2B marketing and AI innovation agency specializing in go-to-market strategy, content development, and generative AI solutions for financial services, fintech, and technology companies." Its fintech content page covers positioning, thought leadership, buyer guides, case studies and AI-search optimization. Its clients page lists Wells Fargo, Citi, FICO and Bankjoy, among others, and says its startup clients "typically include Series A-C fintech and technology companies."

  • Best for: B2B fintechs selling to banks and credit unions that need thought leadership and sales content.
  • Pricing: not published.
  • Fintech proof: a named Bankjoy case study: blogs, whitepapers and nurture content for a digital banking fintech serving credit unions and community banks.
  • Strengths: a long financial-services client list, content plus sales enablement, AI-search work.
  • Limitations: most results on its clients page are anonymized, and the Bankjoy case study reports no metrics. Pricing isn't public.
  • Verdict: the pick for fintechs whose buyers are banks.

7. Siege Media: SEO and GEO content at scale

Siege Media calls itself "the GEO agency that brands hire when ROI isn't optional." 18 of its 84 case studies are tagged fintech (source), including Chime, Stash and Hebbia. Services span content strategy and creation, digital PR, SEO and GEO.

  • Best for: funded fintechs, consumer or B2B, that want large-scale SEO and GEO content, data assets and digital PR, and can commit to a 12-month program.
  • Pricing: "Our minimums for content marketing are currently $8,000 per month" (source).
  • Fintech proof: Chime: "an 82% increase in monthly organic traffic to the blog"; Stash: a 540% increase in organic traffic.
  • Strengths: the deepest fintech SEO case-study library on this list; content, PR and GEO under one roof.
  • Limitations: asks for a 12-month contract (with a 30-day out clause) and doesn't provide full-service social media.
  • Verdict: the pick when organic search is your growth channel and you have the budget for a year of it.

8. NoGood: content, social and paid in one growth team

NoGood describes itself as "The growth squad behind some of the world's most category-defining brands." Its fintech page names Intuit, Chime, Truliant Federal Credit Union, Merlin and Payzer as clients. For Chime, it reports a "93% enrollment lift from LLM traffic."

  • Best for: well-funded consumer fintechs that want SEO and AEO content, organic social and paid media run as one program.
  • Pricing: "Our average retainer is above $20,000/month" (source).
  • Strengths: content, social, paid and CRO in one team; AEO results for a large fintech.
  • Limitations: a full-funnel growth agency rather than a content specialist, and some of its fintech work is paid-led.
  • Verdict: the pick for consumer fintechs with a growth budget.

9. Optimist: SEO and AEO strategy for fintech teams with writers

Optimist says it is "an SEO & AEO consultancy for B2B tech and SaaS companies," and its fintech page offers "Full-Stack SEO & AI Visibility for High-Growth Fintech Companies & Startups." It worked with Stampli "for 7 years from their Series B to Series D rounds," reporting a "5x increase in monthly inbound pipeline."

  • Best for: B2B fintech and financial-software teams with in-house writers that want senior SEO and AEO strategy.
  • Pricing: "Starts at $3,000/mo," month-to-month (source).
  • Strengths: a published, low starting price; long fintech engagements; AEO case studies.
  • Limitations: advisory-led: its About page says "your team owns execution" and refers done-for-you production to partners.
  • Verdict: the pick if you have writers and need the strategy.

Frequently asked questions

What does a fintech content marketing agency do?

It turns a fintech company's expertise and customers into content buyers trust: founder and executive posts, customer stories, guides, reports and search content. The good ones know the products and the buyers, from finance teams to banks, and can show named fintech work you can check.

How much does a fintech content marketing agency cost?

Among the agencies on this list that publish pricing, Optimist starts at $3,000 a month for strategy, StartupCookie at $5,000 a month, Content Rewired at $6,000 a month for a retainer or $7,500 for a sprint, Siege Media at an $8,000 monthly minimum, Mint Studios at roughly $5,000 to $20,000 a month depending on the page, and NoGood at an average retainer above $20,000 a month. Block Club, Fintech Content and Beyond the Arc don't publish pricing.

What should you ask a fintech content agency before hiring?

Ask for named fintech clients and work you can open and read, who will actually write and edit your content, how they fit into your review and approval process, and what they'll publish under your founder's name.

Do fintech startups need a fintech-only agency?

Not always. Fintech-only agencies bring finance vocabulary and buyer context from day one. Generalist B2B agencies can do the job if they've shipped for finance buyers before. Either way, check for named fintech work.
