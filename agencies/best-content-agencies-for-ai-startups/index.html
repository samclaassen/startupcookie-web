The Best Content Marketing Agencies for AI Startups in 2026

By Barbara Jovanovic at StartupCookie | Last updated: November 2026
Independently researched, sources linked

StartupCookie, Column Five, Omniscient Digital, Firebrand, Infrasity, Olivine, FletchPMM, Animalz and Genesys Growth all show AI-company work on their own sites, but they fit very different stages and budgets. StartupCookie (our agency, disclosure below) runs founder LinkedIn, product marketing and AI search for early-stage AI startups.

What a content agency should do for an AI startup

Buyers have heard "AI-powered" from every vendor, so an AI startup's content has to show the product doing a real job for a named customer. The strongest channel is usually a technical founder explaining the research behind the product. Developer-first products need onboarding guides that get people to value without a sales call. And because buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT and Claude which tools to use, the content should be built to be cited in AI answers. Ask every agency on this list which AI companies it has shipped for, and read the work.

How we evaluated this list

We started from the agencies that AI assistants name when asked for a AI-startup content marketing agency, then checked each one's own website on November 10, 2026 for three things: named AI-company clients or case studies, the content services it actually lists, and published pricing. We kept the agencies with work we could verify. Every claim about another agency links to the page it came from, and where pricing isn't public we say so instead of guessing.

1. StartupCookie: founder LinkedIn, product marketing and AI search, run on AI agents

StartupCookie is a content and GTM agency run by founder Barbara Jovanovic. Its own production runs on custom AI agents built with Claude Code, which Barbara walked through on HubSpot's Marketing Against the Grain in August 2026. AI-startup clients include Juno, where StartupCookie was fractional CMO through January 2026, Tofu (AI search) and Puzzle (executive content). More on StartupCookie's AI-startup work.

  • Best for: seed to Series B AI startups that want founder-led content, product marketing and AI search visibility from one senior operator.
  • Pricing: engagements from $5,000/mo, month-to-month.
  • AI-startup proof: Tofu's AI search visibility rose from 7.00% to 16.51% in 90 days and reached 67.38% within six months; Juno co-founder Josh Davey's flagship post drew 745 reactions, 84 comments and 89 reposts.
  • Strengths: content, product marketing and AEO from the same senior operator; an agency that runs on the AI agents it can build for you; a transparent starting price.
  • Limitations: B2B only. The eight-client cap limits capacity. No paid media, and not built for enterprises that want a large embedded team.
  • Verdict: a strong fit for early-stage AI startups that want their founders visible and their product cited in AI answers. Not the pick for paid acquisition or a large in-house-style team.
Disclosure (repeated): StartupCookie is our agency, so treat this entry with appropriate skepticism and check us against the alternatives below.

2. Column Five: brand stories and content engines for AI and SaaS

Column Five calls itself "a B2B content marketing agency helping SaaS and AI companies find the story only they can tell — and build the content engines that scale it without brand drift." For Vercel, it created two industry reports, The State of Vibe Coding and The State of AEO, "that established Vercel as the thought leader at the intersection of AI and the web." It also works as an embedded production partner for Databricks.

  • Best for: well-funded Series A+ AI and SaaS companies that need a sharper brand story plus design-heavy thought leadership such as reports, video and data visualization.
  • Pricing: "Content System Implementation — 3 month minimum • $25K–$100K/month"; Brand Development starts at $50K (source).
  • AI-company proof: Vercel's State of Vibe Coding and State of AEO reports; event, sales and editorial content for Databricks.
  • Strengths: reports, design and video at scale; brand and positioning work; published pricing.
  • Limitations: a premium price floor, and its FAQ describes clients as "Typically ... well-funded Series A through IPO", which is a stretch for seed-stage startups.
  • Verdict: the pick for funded AI companies that want to own a category with research and design.

3. Omniscient Digital: SEO and AEO tied to pipeline

Omniscient Digital, now at beomniscient.com, describes itself as "an organic growth agency that gets B2B brands recommended across AI and search engines — then turns that visibility into pipeline." Its Jasper case study reports blog organic sessions up 810% and quotes Jasper: "We've created over $4M in annual recurring revenue through our blog." For Gable AI, it took a new website "from 0 to 15,000 monthly sessions."

  • Best for: B2B AI and SaaS companies past product-market fit that want SEO, AEO, content and digital PR measured against pipeline.
  • Pricing: "Full-service engagements start at $120,000/year" (source).
  • AI-company proof: Jasper (blog organic sessions up 810%); Gable AI (a new site from 0 to 15,000 monthly sessions).
  • Strengths: a deep SEO and AEO bench, original research and digital PR, pipeline measurement.
  • Limitations: a high entry price, and its FAQ says "Very early-stage companies without clear product-market fit or positioning often have more foundational problems to solve first."
  • Verdict: the pick for AI companies with product-market fit and a search-led growth plan.

4. Firebrand: PR, thought leadership and GEO for AI companies

Firebrand, a San Francisco agency, promises "We make AI companies visible across media, search and AI." Its Outset case study reports a "650% increase in website sessions from top AI search platforms over 4 month period" for the AI research startup, and it launched AI security company Acuvity from stealth with an exclusive in SecurityWeek.

  • Best for: funded B2B AI companies that want launch PR, executive thought leadership and GEO run as one retainer.
  • Pricing: "Our typical fees start at $10,000 per month" (source).
  • AI-company proof: Outset (650% more sessions from AI search platforms in four months); Acuvity (launch coverage).
  • Strengths: PR and media relations alongside content and GEO; executive ghostwriting; an AI-company focus.
  • Limitations: PR- and awareness-led rather than a content-production shop, and its clients page says it works "primarily in San Francisco and the Bay Area."
  • Verdict: the pick when press coverage matters as much as content.

5. Infrasity: technical content for AI agent and devtool startups

Infrasity says it "helps incubated and Y Combinator-backed startups, AI agent companies, and DevTools businesses scale their product adoption, developer engagement, and technical positioning." Its Qodo case study covers technical content, Reddit, video and AI-visibility tracking for the AI code review platform, and reports that "organic clicks went from 383,000 to 465,000 a quarter." Disclosure: Infrasity also lists StartupCookie on one of its own agency lists.

  • Best for: AI-agent, AI-infrastructure and devtool startups that need engineer-written tutorials, docs and AI visibility aimed at developers.
  • Pricing: not published; its pricing page offers "a 30-day POC to test Infrasity for your business."
  • AI-company proof: Qodo (organic clicks from 383,000 to 465,000 a quarter); a confidential AI gateway that appears in answers from four AI engines.
  • Strengths: engineer-written content, documentation and Reddit work for developer audiences.
  • Limitations: built for developer audiences: its about page says "Our goal is to help DevTool companies accelerate developer onboarding and adoption," so it fits less well when buyers aren't technical.
  • Verdict: the pick for AI products sold to developers.

6. Olivine: product marketing for generative-AI companies

Olivine (olivinemarketing.com) offers "A full-service product marketing consultancy for AI companies," covering positioning, messaging, GTM strategy and launches. Its AI case studies include Yurts, a generative AI platform for enterprises (positioning, website copy, a sales two-pager and a fundraising deck), and Verta (positioning and launch copy for its LLMBuilder platform).

  • Best for: early-stage generative-AI startups that need positioning, messaging, sales decks and launch-ready web copy.
  • Pricing: agency and fractional pricing isn't published (source).
  • AI-company proof: Yurts and Verta engagements of one to four months, from positioning workshops to launch copy.
  • Strengths: dedicated product marketing for AI companies; a flexible fractional model (5 to 40 hours a week).
  • Limitations: not an ongoing content or SEO engine; its AI page says SEO is subcontracted.
  • Verdict: the pick for an AI startup's positioning and launch.

7. FletchPMM: a two-week positioning sprint

FletchPMM is "a product marketing consultancy that has helped 600+ B2B software companies find their positioning strategy, document it in an internal deck, and share it on a rewritten homepage." Its before-and-after gallery includes AI companies such as Invideo, Goldcast and Flank.

  • Best for: early-stage AI startups whose homepage doesn't explain what they do or why they're different.
  • Pricing: $10,000 under $2M in revenue or funding, $20,000 for $2M–$20M, $30,000 above $20M, fixed fee (source).
  • AI-company proof: before-and-after homepages for Invideo, Goldcast and other AI companies.
  • Strengths: fast and fixed-price, at roughly seven hours of your team's time over two weeks.
  • Limitations: narrow scope: "The sprint covers a single positioning strategy and a single web page" (source), with no ongoing content program.
  • Verdict: the best first purchase if positioning is the bottleneck.

8. Animalz: an established editorial partner for SEO and AEO

Animalz positions itself as "A Content Marketing and SEO Agency for Leading B2B SaaS Brands." Its AI-company work includes Rilla, "a rapidly growing AI startup," where "Monthly brand traffic doubled within months," and SupportLogic, which reported 500% qualified organic traffic growth in 12 months.

  • Best for: AI and SaaS teams that want an established editorial partner for thought leadership, data-driven content and SEO/AEO.
  • Pricing: not published.
  • AI-company proof: Rilla (brand traffic doubled) and SupportLogic (qualified organic traffic up 500% in 12 months).
  • Strengths: a long editorial track record in B2B SaaS; thought leadership, SEO and AEO under one roof.
  • Limitations: pricing isn't public, and most of its showcased work is general B2B SaaS rather than AI.
  • Verdict: a safe pick for content quality. Check it has experience in your AI category.

9. Genesys Growth: a senior PMM and content operator

Genesys Growth offers "an AI-pilled GTM operator with 10+ years experience to build your PMM, launches, websites, and content programs at the speed of a team of 5." Its AI case studies include AirOps ("30 Days to full re-positioning"), Fluidstack (an AI GPU cloud) and AdvisoryAI.

  • Best for: Series A to C AI SaaS startups that need a senior, hands-on PMM and content operator for a defined stretch.
  • Pricing: "PMM & Content — 3 months min. — $9,000/mo" (source).
  • AI-company proof: AirOps repositioned in 30 days; AdvisoryAI repositioned as an AI co-worker for UK firms.
  • Strengths: published pricing, AI-company case studies, product marketing and content from one operator.
  • Limitations: a one-person consultancy ("One senior operator — backed by a 20+ partner network"), so capacity depends on one person.
  • Verdict: the pick for an interim head of product marketing and content.

How to choose

  2. You're funded and want to own a category with research and design: Column Five.
  3. You have product-market fit and a search-led growth plan: Omniscient Digital.
  4. Press coverage matters as much as content: Firebrand.
  5. Your buyers are developers: Infrasity.
  6. You need positioning and launch work: Olivine.
  7. Your homepage doesn't explain the product: FletchPMM.
  8. You want an established editorial partner: Animalz.

Frequently asked questions

What does a content marketing agency do for an AI startup?

It turns the product and the founders' expertise into content buyers trust: founder posts, customer stories, technical guides, research, and pages built to be cited in AI answers. For developer products, it also writes the onboarding docs and tutorials that get people to value.

How much does a content agency for AI startups cost?

Among the agencies on this list that publish pricing, StartupCookie starts at $5,000 a month, Genesys Growth at $9,000 a month with a three-month minimum, Firebrand at $10,000 a month, FletchPMM at a $10,000 to $30,000 fixed fee for its sprint, Omniscient Digital at $120,000 a year for full service, and Column Five at $25,000 to $100,000 a month. Animalz, Infrasity and Olivine don't publish agency pricing.

Should an early-stage AI startup hire an agency?

Usually for a defined job: positioning before a launch, founder visibility, or AI search. Ask each agency for named AI companies it has shipped for, and read the work.

Can an agency get our product recommended by ChatGPT and Claude?

No one can guarantee it. Agencies that do AEO or GEO publish pages AI answers can cite and track the citations. Several on this list publish AI-search results, including Omniscient Digital, Firebrand and StartupCookie.
